What We Do

Our Detailing Services

From a quick refresh to full multi-stage paint correction — every service is carried out with premium products and genuine care for your vehicle.

Exterior Valet

Snow-foam pre-wash, safe two-bucket hand wash, decontamination, wheels & arches, and a protective sealant finish.

Interior Valet

Deep vacuum, steam sanitisation, upholstery & carpet shampoo, leather cleaning & conditioning, and streak-free glass.

Full Detail (In & Out)

Our signature top-to-bottom service — complete interior deep clean paired with a full exterior wash, decontamination & finish.

Paint Correction

Stage 1–3 machine polishing to remove swirls, oxidation and light defects, restoring gloss and depth to your paintwork.

Scratch & Swirl Removal

Targeted paint correction to reduce or remove scratches, swirl marks and light scuffs — from foxes to shopping trolleys.

Wheel Refurbishment

Kerb rash and scuff repair, diamond-cut & painted finishes, plus deep wheel & arch decontamination.

Ceramic Coating & Protection

Long-lasting ceramic protection that beads water, guards against UV & contamination, and keeps that just-detailed shine.

Vehicle Customising

Personalise your car's look with tasteful styling and finishing touches, carried out to the same detail-first standard as our valets.

Motorworks

Beyond cosmetic care, our team can support light mechanical & motorworks needs — ask us what we can do for your vehicle.

Detailing Courses

Learn the craft with hands-on training run by King Studios — ideal whether you're detailing-curious or starting a career in it.
